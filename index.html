Ship Chart Maker

Instructions
  1. Specify the desired number of characters (3-50).
  2. Upload one or more images to represent the characters, then click "Upload."
    • By default, images are arranged clockwise around the circle, starting at the 12 o'clock position (character 1).
    • To upload images individually, select one file at a time and increment the "starting from 1" value for each upload.
  3. Choose a line type by clicking its color swatch/legend.
  4. Click two character images to connect them with a line.
    • Click a line to remove it.
    • Multiple lines of different colors can connect the same two characters.
